# Skill Path — Leaving the Planet

Gridless's skill web has eight grounded disciplines (Survival, Medical, Combat,
Trade/Guilds, Blacksmithing, Engineering, Carpentry, Magic), each running from a
basic skill up to a branch-defining Keystone, all growing via skill-via-use rather
than points spent at creation (see the Character Creation & Stats section of
`game-overview.md`). This document specs the endgame convergence layer sitting above
all eight: the actual path off the planet.

Design goal: an unskilled colonist should be able to reach space, but no single build
should be forced to grind every discipline to get there. Whichever branch(es) a
player actually invested in should open a legitimate, distinct route — not a single
required checklist.

## The Gateway

**Ruins of the Old Engineers** — Buried beneath the wilderness: the launch complex of
whoever actually built this world. Reaching any one branch's Keystone (Master
Forager, Master Physician, Master Warlord, Master Financier, Master Smith, Master
Engineer, Master Builder, or Master of the Lineage) reveals its existence and grants
access. This is deliberately an *any-one-of-eight* gate, not an all-of-eight gate —
deep investment in a single discipline is enough to discover that the ruins exist.
What you can actually *do* once you're inside depends on which route below you
qualify for.

## The Four Routes

Getting from the Ruins to orbit forks into four mechanically and narratively
distinct routes. A character can qualify for more than one; only one is needed.

### 1. Orbital Engineering — the purist's route
**Requires:** Master Smith (Blacksmithing) + Master Engineer (Engineering) + Master
Builder (Carpentry), in addition to the Ruins gateway.

Design, machine, and build a launch vehicle from first principles — nothing
borrowed, nothing owed. The most demanding route: it requires Keystone-level
investment across three separate crafting disciplines (metal components, systems/
automation, and structural construction), reflecting that building a rocket from
scratch touches every physical trade at once. This is the "did it all myself" route.

### 2. Arcane Propulsion — the mystic's route
**Requires:** Master of the Lineage (Magic) + Kinetic Channeling (Magic, mid-tier
node), in addition to the Ruins gateway.

Your magical lineage, pushed far enough, can substitute for a rocket engine — if you
trust it with your life. The most specialized route: entirely self-contained within
the Magic branch, no crafting or trade dependency at all. This is the route for a
character who went all-in on their lineage rather than spreading investment across
crafting trades.

### 3. Chartered Expedition — the merchant's route
**Requires:** Master Financier (Trade/Guilds) + Guild Apprenticeship (Trade/Guilds,
mid-tier node), in addition to the Ruins gateway.

You don't need to build the ship — you need to fund whoever already can, and hold
the contract. The lowest personal-craft-investment route: it substitutes capital and
guild leverage for hands-on mastery of engineering or magic. This is the route for a
character who built an economic empire rather than a workshop.

### 4. Conquered Launch Site — the warlord's route
**Requires:** Master Warlord (Combat/Warbands) + Siege Tactics (Combat/Warbands,
mid-tier node), in addition to the Ruins gateway.

Somewhere on this replica Earth, a launch facility already stands. Taking it is
faster than building one. The most violent and narratively riskiest route — it's a
shortcut bought with force rather than mastery, and (per the Settlement Warfare
system) should carry real Fame consequences for the Warband that pulls it off.

## Convergence

- **Escape Velocity** — Requires *any one* of the four routes above. Whichever road
  brought a player here, the physics from this point on are the same. This is the
  single narrow point every build passes through, regardless of path.
- **Ascend to the Stars** — Requires Escape Velocity. Liftoff. The replica was never
  the whole world — only the part they let you land on.

## Open Questions

- Are the four routes mutually exclusive per-character, or can a player who
  qualifies for more than one choose freely at the Escape Velocity step?
- Does reaching Ascend to the Stars end a character's arc, unlock a new layer of
  play (orbit, other replica-Earth servers, the "real" world beyond the
  simulation?), or is it a repeatable/server-wide milestone?
- Conquered Launch Site needs a ruling on Fame fallout, consistent with the
  Settlement Warfare capture/destroy consequences already defined in
  `design-brief.md`.
- Does Chartered Expedition require the funding character to personally travel, or
  can a purely economic playstyle "win" without ever leaving a city?
