# Gridless — Game Overview

## 1. Introduction & Setting
Gridless is an immersive survival, world-building, and economic management game set on an artificially constructed, nearly identical replica of Earth created by a mysterious advanced civilization. The player begins as a survivor from a colonist transport ship that suffered catastrophic mechanical failure during reentry.

Upon crash-landing, the player escapes into an untouched wilderness equipped with minimal starting gear: a durable metal canteen, a military-grade survival knife, and a small cache of survival rations.

## 2. Core Pillars of Gameplay

### Survival Phase & Environmental Considerations
- **Immediate Needs:** Finding clean water sources to fill the canteen, foraging, and building primitive shelters using initial starting gear.
- **The Illusion of Earth:** Navigating a world that looks and feels like Earth, but operates on clockwork environmental parameters hinting at its artificial construction.
- **Technological Discrepancy:** Stumbling upon scattered, low-tech agrarian villages populated by descendants of earlier colonization efforts who have regressed to a pre-industrial era.
- **Off-Grid Infrastructure & Climate:** Managing power systems like solar arrays and geothermal loops, regulating internal compound temperatures, and adapting to artificial weather cycles.

### World-Building & Modular Real Estate
Players are not restricted to predefined structures. The game features a flexible real estate and building system:
- **Acquisition Options:** Players can rent, buy, or construct structures for living, crafting, and commerce.
- **The "Equip-to-Define" System:** Empty architectural shells transform based on equipment installed inside them (e.g., metalworking workshops, inns, or medical clinics).
- **Custom Compounds:** Designing specialized zones including living quarters, garages, industrial yards, and medical facilities.

### Economy & Trade System
The game features a robust economic engine blending standardized currency with volatile assets and connected banking:

| Denomination | Value Ratio |
|---|---|
| Copper Coin | 1 Base Unit |
| Iron Coin | 10 Copper |
| Silver Coin | 10 Iron (100 Copper) |
| Gold Coin | 10 Silver (1,000 Copper) |

- **Volatile Gem Market:** Gems act as a high-risk, high-reward asset class with prices fluctuating based on regional supply and demand.
- **Connected Central Banking:** Larger cities feature connected bank branches allowing safe deposit, storage, and digital withdrawals.

### Combat, Damage & Medical Systems
Players face physical threats from NPC factions, hostile player interactions, and aggressive artificial wildlife:
- **Combat Disciplines:** Progression in martial arts, melee weapons, and ranged combat scales directly through active use and practice.
- **Medical Skill Tree:** Healing and medical abilities are learned and improved through application. Treating wounds, performing first aid, and crafting medical supplies elevate medical mastery.
- **Infirmaries & NPC Medics:** Players can equip medical facilities within their compounds, stocking them with specialized gear and hiring trained NPC medics to treat injuries.

### Sociopolitical Systems: Factions & Merchant Guilds
Collective enterprise and sociopolitical structures shape regional dynamics:
- **Player-Created Factions:** Players can band together to establish autonomous factions, securing territorial control, fortifying shared regional compounds, managing collective infrastructure, and engaging in regional rivalries or conflicts over resources and gem markets.
- **Merchant Guilds:** Institutional guilds operate across major settlements, offering structured apprenticeships for advanced crafting tiers, exclusive trade contracts, preferential exchange rates for volatile assets like gems, and guild-backed caravan protection.

### Automation & NPC Management
As players scale up their empire, they can hire specialized NPC villagers to run workshops, manage inns, handle store counters, provide security, and operate medical facilities.

### Magical Evolution & Mastery
Magic is real as a side effect of the world's artificial creation:
- **Magical Lineages:** Players evolve into random magical ability families (Elemental, Illusion, Kinetic, Restoration). Restoration magic integrates directly with medical capabilities to heal wounds.
- **Granular Progression:** Abilities start minute and require deliberate training to master over time.

### Universal Crafting, Disassembly & Intellectual Property
The game features a fully interactive fabrication and innovation ecosystem:
- **Universal Crafting Ladder:** Everything in the game can be crafted, progressing across Crude, Standard, and Mastery tiers.
- **Reverse Engineering & Manuals:** Players can take apart items to learn schematics and write instructional manuals or grimoires to mentor others and NPCs.
- **Research & Patents:** Inventing new items grants official patents and ongoing licensing royalties.
